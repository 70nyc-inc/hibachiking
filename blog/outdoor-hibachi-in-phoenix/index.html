Outdoor hibachi in Phoenix

Patio dinners in Arcadia, Biltmore, Tempe, Chandler, and Gilbert.

Outdoor hibachi in Phoenix is a patio party. The grill is set outside at houses in Arcadia, the Biltmore area, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, and other East Valley addresses we can confirm. Guests stay in the yard for the flames and the knife work, then eat the plated dinner there or at a table you have already set.

A patio that can hold the grill

The chef needs a safe outdoor spot: backyard, terrace, or under an awning. Covered patios are common and they work when the chef can cook outside rather than in a closed room. You provide tables, chairs, plates, and utensils. The visit includes the grill, the food, and the show. Setup is usually a few minutes after arrival.

East Valley versus central Phoenix

Arcadia and Biltmore are not the same drive as Chandler or Gilbert. Tempe sits between them. Send the actual address so the date and any location adjustment are based on that street, not on the word Phoenix. We do not treat every East Valley town as automatically on the route.

Price in the yard

$50 per adult and $25 per child 12 and under, with a $500 minimum. Gratuity is separate, suggested at 20%, and the price can change with location. Two proteins, salad, vegetables, and fried rice are the plate. Scallops and filet are +$5. Lobster tail is +$15. Thirty or more adults means two chefs. Cash on the day. No deposit.

Reserve Phoenix

Book from the Phoenix page. Email confirms the hold. About six days before, support checks the details. Mention allergies, gluten-free sauces, or tofu then. No nuts or sesame are used. Text (929) 992-9932 if you want the headcount checked before you book.
