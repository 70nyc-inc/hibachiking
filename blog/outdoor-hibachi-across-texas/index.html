Outdoor hibachi across Texas

Four cities, one menu. The address decides which page to book.

Outdoor hibachi across Texas runs through four cities: Austin, Dallas, Fort Worth, and Houston. The plate is the same in each, at $50 per person, $25 for children 12 and under, and a $500 minimum. What changes is the drive, the kind of yard, and which booking page holds the date.

Pick the city that matches the house

Austin covers central neighborhoods plus Round Rock, Cedar Park, Westlake, Lakeway, and Bee Cave. Dallas covers Uptown, Highland Park, Preston Hollow, Plano, Frisco, Richardson, Las Colinas, and Allen. Fort Worth covers the Cultural District, TCU, Southlake, Keller, Alliance, and Westover Hills. Houston covers River Oaks, the Heights, Memorial, Montrose, Katy, Sugar Land, and The Woodlands when the date is open.

Book the city that has the house

A Frisco party belongs on the Dallas page. A Southlake party belongs on Fort Worth. A Woodlands party belongs on Houston. Using the nearest city name is how the address gets confirmed and how a location adjustment, if any, is quoted. Gratuity is not included. Cash is due the day of the party. Thirty or more adults need two chefs.

Read the city note

Each city has its own article for the local setup, then a landing page with the calendar. Start with the note if you are choosing between two Texas cities, then reserve on the page that names the town where the grill will actually be.
