Pennsylvania

Outdoor hibachi across Pennsylvania

Philadelphia and nearby suburbs. Not a promise for the whole state.

Outdoor hibachi across Pennsylvania is a Philadelphia-area service, plus nearby suburbs when the address can be confirmed. It is not a standing route to every Pennsylvania city. Availability and any location adjustment come from the street where the party will be.

What the local parties look like

Some are smaller city yards. Some are suburban lawns with room for the grill and a long table. In both cases the chef cooks outdoors and the host provides tables, chairs, plates, and utensils. The menu is the standard plate: two proteins, salad, vegetables, and fried rice, at $50 per person with a $500 minimum.

Book with the address

Use the Pennsylvania page and include the town. A New Jersey address should not be forced onto this calendar. Confirmation is email, with a check-in about six days before. Cash on the day, no deposit, gratuity not included.

Read the city note

The Pennsylvania article covers the difference between a Philadelphia yard and a suburban one, plus allergies and upgrades. Start there if you are not sure the suburb is close enough.
