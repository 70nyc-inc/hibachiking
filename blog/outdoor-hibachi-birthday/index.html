Birthdays

An outdoor hibachi birthday

A birthday in the yard, with the flames and the full plate, without a restaurant wait.

An outdoor hibachi birthday is the same service as any other party: one chef, one grill, and the same plate for every guest. The difference is the reason people are in the yard.

What guests eat

Each person picks two proteins from chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu. The plate also has salad, hibachi vegetables, and fried rice. Add gyoza ($10) or edamame ($5) if you want something before the grill starts.

Kids

Children 12 and under are $25 and eat the same menu. The $500 minimum still applies to the party, so a small kids' table does not replace it.

Keep the list private

You choose who comes. The chef cooks outside and leaves the seating plan to you. Book the city, then send the birthday headcount if it changes. Support usually checks in about six days before the party.
