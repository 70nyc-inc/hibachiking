Miami

Outdoor hibachi in Miami

Brickell terraces, Coral Gables yards, Miami Beach, and a safe spot by the pool.

Outdoor hibachi in Miami is booked by neighborhood, because a Brickell terrace, a Coral Gables house, and a Miami Beach rental are different setups. Doral, Aventura, and Kendall are on the list when the date is open. The show works on a patio, or beside a pool, only when the chef has a safe outdoor place for the grill.

High-rise, house, or rental

A tower terrace needs enough floor for the grill and a clear way to bring it up. A house in Coral Gables or Kendall usually has a yard. A vacation rental needs the same thing every other party needs from the host: tables, chairs, plates, and utensils. The chef does not supply the dining room. The chef does bring the grill, the food, and the show, and cooks outside.

The Miami plate

Salad, hibachi vegetables, fried rice, and two proteins per person. Chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu are included at $50. Children 12 and under are $25. The minimum is $500. Scallops and filet are +$5, lobster tail is +$15. Parties of 30 or more adults are scheduled with two chefs, which is worth deciding before you hold a large Miami Beach date.

Payment and allergies

Cash on the day of the event. No deposit. Gratuity is not included, and 20% is the suggestion. The price can vary by location. No nuts or sesame. Ask for gluten-free soy and teriyaki when you book. Tofu covers vegetarian and vegan guests at the regular rate.

Miami's calendar

Miami has its own appointment calendar. Reserve there, then watch email for confirmation. About six days before, support contacts you. If the rental address or the headcount changes, text (929) 992-9932. Do not assume a pool deck is approved until the outdoor spot is described.
