Houston

Outdoor hibachi in Houston

Inside the city, or a drive out to Katy, Sugar Land, and The Woodlands.

Outdoor hibachi in Houston has to say which part of the city, because River Oaks, the Heights, Memorial, and Montrose are not the same booking as Katy, Sugar Land, or The Woodlands. The menu is the same. The address tells us whether the date is open and whether the price changes with the drive.

Cook outside, eat inside

Houston parties often keep the guests in the air conditioning and the grill on the patio. That fits the service: the chef cooks outdoors, on a backyard, balcony, terrace, or under an awning, and the plates can move inside. The host provides the furniture and the place settings. The chef brings the hibachi grill, the food, and the show.

The plate

Each guest gets two proteins, a salad, hibachi vegetables, and fried rice. Chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu are in the base price of $50. A third protein is extra, noodles are +$4, and a side of fried rice is +$4. Vegetarian and vegan guests take tofu at the same per-person rate, with extra vegetables, salad, and rice as needed.

Suburbs and headcount

Katy, Sugar Land, and The Woodlands are served when the date is open, not as a standing promise for every Saturday. Send the street address with the request. Children 12 and under are $25, and the party minimum is $500. Thirty or more adults need two chefs. Gratuity is not included. Payment is cash that day, with no deposit.

Book the Houston date

Start on the Houston page. After you reserve, look for the confirmation email. About six days before, support confirms details. Mention allergies then. The kitchen does not use nuts or sesame, and gluten-free soy and teriyaki can be brought if you ask ahead. Questions go to (929) 992-9932.
