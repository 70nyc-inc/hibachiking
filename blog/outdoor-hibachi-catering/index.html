Catering

Outdoor hibachi catering

A private chef, a grill, and dinner in the yard. Here is what the visit includes.

Outdoor hibachi catering is a chef cooking at your house, not a table at a steakhouse. Hibachi King carries the grill, the ingredients, and the teppanyaki show. You keep the guest list and set the table.

What the chef brings

The visit includes a mobile hibachi grill, salad, hibachi vegetables, fried rice, and two proteins per guest. Chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu are in the base price. Scallops and filet mignon are +$5. Lobster tail is +$15. Noodles are +$4.

Where it can be cooked

The grill stays outside: backyard, balcony, terrace, or under an awning. Guests can eat outside with the show or sit inside while the chef cooks out. The team is licensed and insured.

Where Hibachi King cooks

Outdoor hibachi catering is booked in Austin, Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Miami, Orlando, and Phoenix. Pick the city page for neighborhoods, then hold the date on that city's calendar.
