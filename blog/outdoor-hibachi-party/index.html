Planning an outdoor hibachi party

Headcount, the outdoor spot for the grill, and what the host still sets up.

An outdoor hibachi party works when the grill has a safe spot and the host already has tables. Hibachi King does not bring chairs, plates, or utensils. Those stay with the house.

How many guests

Price the party at $50 per adult and $25 per child 12 and under, with a $500 minimum. At 30 adults or more, schedule two chefs so the show and the plates stay on time.

What to send before the date

The address, the headcount, protein choices, and any allergy. The food has no nuts or sesame. Gluten-free soy and teriyaki can be packed if you say so when you book. Tofu is the vegetarian and vegan plate, at the same per-person price.

The day of

The chef arrives at the reservation time. Setup usually takes a few minutes. Payment is cash that day. There is no deposit. Gratuity is not included; 20% is the suggestion.
