Orlando

Outdoor hibachi in Orlando

Vacation rentals and reunion weekends, from Dr. Phillips to Kissimmee.

Outdoor hibachi in Orlando is often a vacation rental rather than the host's own house. Family reunions, birthday weekends, and trip celebrations book around Dr. Phillips, Winter Park, Lake Nona, and Kissimmee. The chef cooks at that address. The group skips a crowded restaurant and keeps the list private.

What a rental still has to have

The chef brings the grill and the food. The rental has to cover tables, chairs, plates, and utensils, same as a private home. If the house is short on place settings, that is the host's job before the chef arrives. The grill goes in the backyard, on a patio, or under an awning. It does not come indoors. Guests can eat inside.

Reunions and kids

A reunion weekend mixes adults and children. Adults are $50, children 12 and under are $25, and the $500 minimum applies to the whole party. Each person picks two proteins: chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, or tofu. At 30 adults, plan on two chefs so a long table does not wait on one grill.

Allergies on a trip

Tell us when you book, not at the door. There are no nuts or sesame. Gluten-free soy and teriyaki can be packed for the party. Tofu is the vegetarian and vegan plate at the same per-person price, with extra vegetables, salad, and fried rice as needed. Gratuity is separate, suggested at 20%. Cash on the day. No deposit.

Book Orlando

Use the Orlando page and put the rental address in the notes if the form allows, or send it when support writes about six days before. Confirmation is email. The price can change with the location, so Kissimmee and Winter Park should not be treated as the same drive. Call or text (929) 992-9932 with changes.
