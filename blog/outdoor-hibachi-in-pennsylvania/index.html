Pennsylvania

Outdoor hibachi in Pennsylvania

Philadelphia and nearby suburbs. The street address decides the date.

Outdoor hibachi in Pennsylvania is centered on Philadelphia and nearby suburbs, not on every town in the state. A city yard and a suburban backyard are both possible. Which one can be served on your date depends on the street address, so that address is the first thing to send, along with the headcount.

City yard or suburban lawn

A Philadelphia house may have a small yard, a patio, or only a spot under cover. A suburban party usually has more room for the grill and the guests. Both are outdoor cooking. The chef does not move the grill indoors. Guests can still eat inside if that is how the house is set. You provide tables, chairs, plates, and utensils.

The menu stays plain

Two proteins from chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu. Salad, hibachi vegetables, and fried rice on the plate. Scallops and filet mignon are +$5. Lobster tail is +$15. Noodles are +$4. The base rate is $50 per person and $25 for a child 12 and under, with a $500 minimum. Gratuity is extra.

Tell the kitchen before the day

The food does not contain nuts or sesame. Other allergies need a note when you book. Gluten-free soy and teriyaki can be packed. Tofu is available at the same price. If the party is 30 adults or more, two chefs are required. Payment is cash on the day. There is no deposit. Location can change the price, which is another reason the Philadelphia address has to be specific.

Reserve Pennsylvania

Book from the Pennsylvania page and include where the party will be. Confirmation is by email. Support usually reaches out about six days ahead. Call or text (929) 992-9932 if the suburb or the guest count changes after that.
