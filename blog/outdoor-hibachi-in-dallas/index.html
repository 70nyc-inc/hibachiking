Dallas

Outdoor hibachi in Dallas

Uptown terraces, north-Dallas yards, and larger parties in Plano and Frisco.

Outdoor hibachi in Dallas splits into two kinds of address. Uptown is often a terrace. Highland Park and Preston Hollow are backyard dinners. Plano, Frisco, Richardson, Allen, and Las Colinas are where the headcount tends to grow, and that is the detail to send when you book, because the chef count follows the number of adults.

Terrace or a full yard

A terrace still works. The grill stays outdoors, and guests can sit inside if the seating is there. A Preston Hollow or Frisco yard gives the show more room, which is nicer when the guest list is long, but it is not a requirement. You provide the tables, chairs, plates, and utensils either way.

When the party needs two chefs

Thirty or more adults means two chefs, so the plates and the show stay on time. That comes up often on Plano and Frisco dates. The base price is still $50 per adult and $25 per child 12 and under, with a $500 minimum. Gratuity is separate. Suggested gratuity is 20%. The final price can vary by the exact address, including a drive north to Allen or Frisco.

What to send with the date

Address, headcount, two proteins per guest, and any allergy. Chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu are included. Scallops and filet mignon are +$5, lobster tail is +$15, and noodles are +$4. There are no nuts or sesame. Gluten-free soy and teriyaki are available if you ask before the day. Tofu replaces meat at the same price.

Booking Dallas

Reserve from the Dallas page. After the calendar hold, check email for confirmation. The team usually contacts you about six days out. Cash is due the day of the party, with no deposit. Text (929) 992-9932 if the list changes after you book.
