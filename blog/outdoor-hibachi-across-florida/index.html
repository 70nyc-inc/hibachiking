Outdoor hibachi across Florida

Miami neighborhoods and Orlando rental weekends. Two cities, not the whole state.

Outdoor hibachi across Florida means Miami and Orlando. It does not mean every Florida city. Miami is a neighborhood booking: Brickell, Coral Gables, Miami Beach, Doral, Aventura, and Kendall, often a terrace or a patio with a safe spot for the grill. Orlando is often a vacation rental in Dr. Phillips, Winter Park, Lake Nona, or Kissimmee.

Why the parties feel different

A Miami date is frequently the host's house or a short-stay rental near the water, with the guest list already in town. An Orlando date is frequently a reunion or a birthday weekend where everyone is staying in one house. Both still need the host to set tables, chairs, plates, and utensils. The chef brings the grill and cooks outside.

Same price, separate calendars

$50 per person, $25 for kids 12 and under, $500 minimum, gratuity extra. Miami has its own appointment calendar. Orlando is booked from the Orlando page. Do not use one city's hold for the other city's address. The price can change with location. Cash on the day, no deposit, and two chefs at 30 adults.

Which note to read

Read the Miami note if the party is a patio, a tower terrace, or a pool deck that still needs a safety check. Read the Orlando note if the address is a rental and you need to plan place settings before the chef arrives.
