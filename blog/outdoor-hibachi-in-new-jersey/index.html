Outdoor hibachi in New Jersey

North Jersey and Central Jersey backyards. The town is confirmed from the address.

Outdoor hibachi in New Jersey is a backyard party. Hibachi King cooks for North Jersey and Central Jersey when the town checks out from the street address. It is not a statewide route, and it is not a restaurant reservation. The chef brings a grill, salad, vegetables, fried rice, and two protein choices per guest.

Why the town matters

Two New Jersey parties on the same Saturday can be a very different drive. We confirm the town before the event so the date and any location adjustment are real. Put the full address in the booking. A backyard is the usual setup. A patio or a spot under an awning also works. The grill stays outside.

What you set out

Tables, chairs, plates, and utensils stay with the house. The chef does not bring a dining room. If some guests will eat inside, set that table as well. The show can remain in the yard. Licensed and insured, same as the other cities.

Price

$50 per person, $25 for children 12 and under, $500 minimum. Suggested gratuity is 20% and is not included. At 30 adults, schedule two chefs. Cash only, paid the day of the party, with no deposit. The price may vary by location once the town is confirmed.

Book New Jersey

New Jersey has its own calendar. Hold the date there, then check email. About six days out, support goes over headcount and allergies. No nuts or sesame in the food. Gluten-free soy and teriyaki on request. Tofu at the regular per-person price for vegetarian and vegan guests. The phone is (929) 992-9932.
