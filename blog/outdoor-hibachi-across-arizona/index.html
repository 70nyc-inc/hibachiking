Outdoor hibachi across Arizona

Phoenix patios and the East Valley. One metro, not the whole state.

Outdoor hibachi across Arizona is centered on Phoenix patios and nearby East Valley parties. Arcadia, the Biltmore area, Tempe, Chandler, and Gilbert are the places named on the Phoenix page. Other Arizona cities are not a separate service. An address still has to be confirmed before it is treated as on the route.

Why it is a patio service

The party is built for the yard. The chef sets the grill outside. Guests watch the show there. A covered patio works when the cooking itself stays outdoors. The host provides the table setting. This is the same rule as every other city, and it matters on patios that were not planned as a dining room.

Price

$50 per adult, $25 per child 12 and under, $500 minimum. Gratuity suggested at 20% and not included. The price can change with location, so a Gilbert address is not automatically the Arcadia price. Cash on the day. No deposit. Two chefs at 30 adults.
