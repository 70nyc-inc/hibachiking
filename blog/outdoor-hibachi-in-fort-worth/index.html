Fort Worth

Outdoor hibachi in Fort Worth

In-town yards around TCU and the Cultural District, plus Southlake and Keller.

Outdoor hibachi in Fort Worth uses the same chef, grill, and plate as Dallas and Austin, but the drive is the west side of the metro. Cultural District and TCU parties are in town. Southlake, Keller, Alliance, and Westover Hills are a different trip, so the street address decides timing and whether a location adjustment applies.

The outdoor spot

Backyard, patio, balcony, terrace, or under an awning. The grill does not come inside. If the group would rather eat in the house, set that table too. The show stays at the grill. Setup is usually a few minutes once the chef is on site. You still put out tables, chairs, plates, and utensils.

Weekends and birthdays

Fort Worth dates are often a birthday or a weekend dinner for family around those neighborhoods. The menu is per person: salad, hibachi vegetables, fried rice, and two proteins from chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu. Upgrades are scallops or filet at +$5 and lobster tail at +$15. Edamame is $5 and gyoza is $10 if you want a plate before the grill starts.

Price on the Fort Worth side

$50 per person, $25 for a child 12 and under, $500 minimum. Gratuity is not in that number. Twenty percent is the suggestion. Parties of 30 or more adults are scheduled with two chefs. Payment is cash on the day, and there is no deposit. Because Southlake and Alliance are not the same drive as a TCU rental, mention the town when you ask about the date.

Reserve Fort Worth

Open the Fort Worth page and hold the date there. Confirmation comes by email, and support typically checks in about six days before. Allergies, gluten-free soy sauce, and a tofu plate should be on that note. Call or text (929) 992-9932 if the headcount moves.
