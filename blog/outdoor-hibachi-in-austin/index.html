Austin

Outdoor hibachi in Austin

A home market, from central patios out to Round Rock, Cedar Park, and the lake.

Outdoor hibachi in Austin is one of Hibachi King's home markets, so the dates cover houses inside the city and yards north and west of it. Downtown and South Congress parties are often a patio or a small yard. Round Rock, Cedar Park, Westlake, Lakeway, and Bee Cave usually have more lawn, which changes where the grill can sit, not the menu.

Where the grill goes

The chef cooks outside. A Domain terrace, a South Congress patio, and a Lakeway backyard all qualify when there is a safe spot for the grill. Guests can eat at the grill or at a table inside. The host still sets tables, chairs, plates, and utensils. The chef brings the grill, the food, and the show.

The parties people book

Austin dates are often birthdays, graduations, and bachelor or bachelorette weekends, plus a Friday dinner that would otherwise have been a restaurant reservation. The plate does not change with the occasion: salad, hibachi vegetables, fried rice, and two proteins. Chicken, steak, shrimp, salmon, and tofu are in the $50 price. Scallops and filet are +$5. Lobster tail is +$15.

Headcount and the day

Children 12 and under are $25. Every party has a $500 minimum. At 30 adults, book two chefs. Gratuity is not included, and 20% is the suggestion. The price can change with the address, which matters when the party is in Bee Cave or Lakeway rather than central Austin. Payment is cash on the day. There is no deposit.

How to reserve Austin

Use the Austin page to hold the date, then watch email for the confirmation. Support usually writes about six days before the party. If the headcount or the protein list changes, call or text (929) 992-9932. Tell us then about allergies. The food has no nuts or sesame, gluten-free soy and teriyaki can be packed, and tofu is the vegetarian plate at the same per-person price.
