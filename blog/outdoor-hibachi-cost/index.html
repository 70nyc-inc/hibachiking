Pricing

What outdoor hibachi costs

The base price, the upgrades, and what is not on the check.

The outdoor hibachi cost starts at $50 per person and $25 per child 12 and under. Every party has a $500 minimum, even when the headcount is small.

Included

Two proteins, salad, fried rice, hibachi vegetables, the grill, and the live show. The chef brings those. Tables, chairs, plates, and utensils are not included.

Extras

Scallops +$5, filet mignon +$5, lobster tail +$15. A side of fried rice or noodles is +$4. An extra protein is +$10, a filet side is +$15, and a lobster side is +$20. Edamame is $5 and gyoza is $10.

Not included

Gratuity is not included. The suggestion is 20%. The price can change with the address. Payment is cash on the day of the party, with no deposit. Use the estimate page for a headcount, then book the city to hold the date.
