Outdoor hibachi instead of a restaurant

Same kind of show, at the address you already have.

A restaurant hibachi table is a shared room and a fixed seating time. Outdoor hibachi moves that cooking to your yard, patio, or terrace, and the only guests are the ones you invited.

What stays the same

The plate is still salad, vegetables, fried rice, and two proteins, with a chef cooking in front of the group. Upgrades such as filet and lobster are available if you want them.

What changes

You provide the furniture and the place settings. The chef does not take over the dining room: the grill stays outdoors. People can eat inside while the cooking stays outside, which helps in rain under an awning or on a small New York terrace.

When it is the better fit

Birthdays, graduations, and weekends where the group is already at one house. If you want a private room downtown, this is a different product. If you want the chef at the address on the invitation, book outdoor hibachi in your city.
